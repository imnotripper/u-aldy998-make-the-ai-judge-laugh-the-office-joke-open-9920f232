# Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open

Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) competition on make.fun.

## The joke

> Our team-building exercise was an escape room, and we just stood there in silence like it was a normal standup.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib-only Python, deterministic, no network) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line, no trailing newline, well under the 200-character limit.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this joke

The setup promises teamwork and escape; the payoff is that the team does exactly what it always does in a standup — stand around in silence. The specific office detail is the standup itself, and the recognition lands in the last few words: the "exercise" changed nothing.
